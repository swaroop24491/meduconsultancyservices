Our Story

From a computer training center to a trusted name in EPF and ESI compliance, built over 20+ years.

01

How We Started

Medu Consultancy was founded by Medu Thirumaleshwara Bhat, who brings 35 years of experience working with the Employees' Provident Fund Organization (EPFO). We started as a computer training center, and pivoted to PF and ESI compliance as we saw a growing need for expert guidance in this space.

02

Our Journey

What began as a local service in the coastal towns of Mangalore, Udupi, Puttur, Bantwal, Kundapura, and Gokarna gradually expanded to serve businesses throughout Karnataka, including Bangalore, with comprehensive, reliable, and expert compliance support.

03

Leadership

Medu Thirumaleshwara Bhat, our CEO, leads the company with decades of experience and a passion for helping businesses navigate the complexities of EPF and ESI compliance. His dedication has been the driving force behind our continued growth.

04

Our Mission

We simplify the complexities of EPF and ESI compliance for businesses, ensuring seamless operations and full regulatory compliance — with personalized support, expert knowledge, and vast experience.
