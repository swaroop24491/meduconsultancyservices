Do you need to register for PF?

Yes, if your business has {{ epf.min_employees }} or more staff. Count everyone, including casual staff and staff sent by a contractor. Once PF applies, it keeps applying, even if you later have fewer than {{ epf.min_employees }} staff.

The 20-staff rule

PF (Provident Fund, also called EPF) applies to any business with {{ epf.min_employees }} or more staff, in any industry. Exactly {{ epf.min_employees }} counts.

Once PF applies, it stays, even if your staff later drop below {{ epf.min_employees }}.

Have fewer than {{ epf.min_employees }} staff? PF does not have to apply yet. Call us and we will check your case.

Hire extra staff for a busy season? Count them too. If you reach {{ epf.min_employees }}, call us to check how PF applies to you.

Who counts towards the 20

Count all of these:

  • Permanent staff
  • Casual staff
  • Staff sent by a contractor
  • Staff who earn more than ₹25,000 a month (the PF pay limit)

Do not count: apprentices, the owner (if you are a sole proprietor) and the partners of a partnership firm.

Paid directors? Call us to check how to count them. LLP? Call us to check.

Which staff must join PF

PF is a must for staff whose PF wages are {{ epf.wage_ceiling_text }} a month or less. This is the new limit from {{ epf.wage_ceiling_from }}. Before that it was {{ epf.wage_ceiling_old_text }}.

PF wages are basic pay plus DA (extra pay that rises with prices), not the full salary. If other allowances are more than half the salary, add the extra part.

Above {{ epf.wage_ceiling_text }}? A new joiner can still choose to join. Staff who are already members stay members.

Wage rules changing? Call us. We check this for you.

If you register late

If you should have registered earlier, you may have to pay the PF for the earlier months, with interest and a fine. Register as soon as it applies. Call us and we register you.
