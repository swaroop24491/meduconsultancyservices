What happens if you pay PF late?

EPFO can charge interest and damages (a fine) on top of the PF you owe. Interest is 12% a year. For PF due from 14 June 2024, damages are 1% of the unpaid amount for each month late, up to 100% of it. EPFO decides the final amount.

When PF is due

PF for a month is due by the {{ f.due_day }}th of the next month. For example, PF for March is due by 15 April. Pay after that date and it is late.

Interest

Interest is 12% a year on the unpaid amount. It is counted for each day, from the due date until the day you pay. It is charged on every late payment.

Damages

Damages are a fine on top of the interest. EPFO decides whether to charge them, and how much.

  • PF due on or after 14 June 2024: 1% of the unpaid amount for each month late. Part of a month counts as a full month, so 32 days late counts as 2 months. Damages can't be more than the unpaid amount.
  • PF due before 14 June 2024: a yearly rate that goes up the longer you are late: 5% (under 2 months), 10% (2 to 4 months), 15% (4 to 6 months), 25% (6 months or more).

Was your PF due before 14 June 2024, and paid after it? The rules for this are not clear. Call us to check. For very long delays, call us to check the amount.

Example: ₹50,000 paid 66 days late

You owe ₹50,000 of PF for a month due on or after 14 June 2024. You pay 66 days late.

  • Interest: ₹1,085 (12% a year for 66 days)
  • Damages: ₹1,500 (1% for each of 3 months, as part of a month counts as a full month)
  • Extra to pay: ₹2,585
  • Total you pay: ₹52,585

These are estimates. EPFO decides the final amount.

If an inspection finds it

An inspection can find late payments. You may then have to pay the unpaid amount, interest and damages together. Read what happens in a PF inspection.

How to avoid it

  • Pay your PF by the {{ f.due_day }}th every month.
  • Check the amount with our free PF contribution calculator before you pay.
  • Each month, compare payroll with what you paid, so a missed payment is caught in days, not months.
