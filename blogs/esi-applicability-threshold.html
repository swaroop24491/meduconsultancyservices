Do you need to register for ESI?

Yes, if your business has {{ esi.min_employees }} or more staff. Count everyone, including casual staff and staff sent by a contractor. Once ESI applies, it keeps applying, even if you later have fewer than {{ esi.min_employees }} staff.

Rules checked on {{ site.data.facts.tools_checked }}

The 10-staff rule

ESI (Employees' State Insurance) gives your staff medical care, and pay when they can't work because of sickness, injury or maternity. It applies to any business with {{ esi.min_employees }} or more staff. The rule is the same in every state. Seasonal factories are not covered by the {{ esi.min_employees }}-staff rule.

Once ESI applies, it stays, even if your staff later drop below {{ esi.min_employees }}.

For some risky kinds of work, ESI can apply even with 1 employee. Call us to check.

Who counts towards the 10

Count all of these:

  • Permanent staff
  • Casual staff
  • Staff sent by a contractor
  • Staff who earn more than ₹21,000 a month (the ESI pay limit)

Do not count: apprentices, the owner (if one person owns the business) and the partners of a partnership firm.

Paid directors? Call us to check how to count them.

Which staff must join ESI

ESI is a must for staff whose ESI wages are {{ esi.wage_ceiling_text }} a month or less ({{ esi.wage_ceiling_pwd_text }} for a person with a disability).

ESI wages are not the full pay. House rent allowance (HRA), overtime, bonus, commission and travel allowance are left out. But ESI wages must be at least half the total pay. If they come to less, the difference is added to ESI wages.

Staff on very low daily pay don't pay the employee share, but you still pay yours. Call us and we will check each person.

If you register late

If you should have registered earlier, you may have to pay the ESI for the earlier months, with interest and a fine. Register as soon as it applies. Call us and we register you.
