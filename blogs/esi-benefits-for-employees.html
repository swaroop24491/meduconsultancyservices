What do you get from ESI?

ESI (Employees' State Insurance) gives you medical care, and cash help when you are ill, hurt or having a baby. You pay a small part. Your employer pays most of the cost. If your employer's business has {{ site.data.facts.esi.min_employees }} or more staff and your ESI wages are {{ site.data.facts.esi.wage_ceiling_text }} or less, joining is a must.

Updated 2 October 2026 · Rules checked on {{ site.data.facts.tools_checked }}

What ESI is

ESI is a government insurance scheme for staff. It covers you if your ESI wages are {{ site.data.facts.esi.wage_ceiling_text }} a month or less ({{ site.data.facts.esi.wage_ceiling_pwd_text }} for a person with a disability). ESI wages are not your full pay. House rent allowance (HRA), overtime, bonus, commission and travel allowance are left out. But if these extra items are more than half of your total pay, the extra part is added back.

You pay 0.75% of your ESI wages. Your employer pays 3.25%. Example: ESI wages of ₹18,000 a month. You pay ₹135. Your employer pays ₹585. Check your own amount with our free ESI contribution calculator.

What you get

  • Medical care when you are ill.
  • Cash help when you can't work because of illness.
  • Maternity help for women having a baby.
  • Help if you are hurt at work: medical care and cash. ESI can also help your family if you die because of a work injury.

ESIC sets the rules for each benefit. Call us to check your case.

Why you can't opt out

ESI is a must when your employer's business has {{ site.data.facts.esi.min_employees }} or more staff (some risky kinds of work too) and your ESI wages are {{ site.data.facts.esi.wage_ceiling_text }} a month or less ({{ site.data.facts.esi.wage_ceiling_pwd_text }} for a person with a disability). You can't opt out, and your employer can't leave you out. Staff on very low daily pay don't pay their share, but the employer still pays theirs.

Your pay goes above {{ site.data.facts.esi.wage_ceiling_text }}? Don't stop ESI on your own. Call us to check when it can stop.

How to use ESI

Each covered employee has an IP number (your ESI number). Your employer registers you with ESI when you join. Ask for your IP number.
