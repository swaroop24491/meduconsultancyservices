What does your business get from PF and ESI?

PF is a must with {{ site.data.facts.epf.min_employees }} or more staff, and ESI with {{ site.data.facts.esi.min_employees }} or more. They can also help you keep good staff and look like a steady employer. Pay on time, and you avoid late-payment costs.

Updated 2 October 2026 · Rules checked on {{ site.data.facts.tools_checked }}

Staff who stay longer

PF gives your staff savings and a pension. ESI gives them medical care and cash help. Staff who feel safe are more likely to stay. That can save you the cost of finding and training new people.

Easier hiring

Job seekers may ask about PF and ESI. Offering both can show you run a steady business.

Avoid late-payment costs

PF applies with {{ site.data.facts.epf.min_employees }} or more staff and ESI with {{ site.data.facts.esi.min_employees }} or more. Pay late, and you owe interest and may also owe damages (a fine). Read What paying PF late costs and What paying ESI late costs. Pay by the 15th of the next month, and you avoid interest and damages for late payment.

What it costs you

Your main shares are 12% of PF wages for PF, and 3.25% of ESI wages for ESI. Check your amounts with our free calculators. These are the main shares. Call us to check your full cost.

When staff don't want to join

Some staff want more take-home pay and ask to skip PF or ESI. If they are covered, joining is a must. You can't leave them out, and they can't opt out. It helps to show them what they get. Share these two pages with them: What do you get from PF? and What do you get from ESI?
