PF wage limit is now ₹25,000: what employers must change

From {{ epf.wage_ceiling_from }}, the PF wage limit is {{ epf.wage_ceiling_text }} a month. It was {{ epf.wage_ceiling_old_text }}. So more of your staff may now need to be in PF. Here is who, and what to change.

Last updated {{ page.last_modified | date: "%-d %B %Y" }} · Rules checked on {{ page.rules_checked | date: "%-d %B %Y" }}

What changed on 17 September 2026

PF (Provident Fund, also called EPF) gives your staff savings and a pension. Staff whose PF wages are at or below a limit must be in PF, if the rules cover them. That limit is called the PF wage limit, or wage ceiling.

The limit was {{ epf.wage_ceiling_old_text }} a month from September 2014. The government raised it to {{ epf.wage_ceiling_text }} a month from {{ epf.wage_ceiling_from }}.

PF rules now come from the {{ site.data.facts.law }}, in force from {{ site.data.facts.law_in_force }}, and the EPF Scheme, 2026.

The employee pays {{ epf.employee_rate_pct }}% of their PF wages. You pay {{ epf.employer_rate_pct }}%, split between their PF savings and the pension (EPS). PF is due by the {{ epf.due_day }}th of the next month.

Which staff must now be in PF

Staff whose PF wages are {{ epf.wage_ceiling_text }} a month or less must be in PF, if the rules cover them.

PF wages are basic pay plus DA (dearness allowance: extra pay that rises with prices). They are not the full salary. But PF wages must be at least half the total pay. If they come to less, the difference is added to PF wages.

Example: an employee's basic pay plus DA is ₹20,000 a month, and their total pay is ₹35,000. Half of ₹35,000 is ₹17,500, so their PF wages are ₹20,000. That is more than the old {{ epf.wage_ceiling_old_text }} limit, so if they were not in PF, they did not have to join. It is less than {{ epf.wage_ceiling_text }}, so now they must be in PF. Each month, the employee pays ₹2,400 ({{ epf.employee_rate_pct }}%) and you pay ₹2,400 ({{ epf.employer_rate_pct }}%) as your PF share. This is an estimate. Check your own figures with our PF contribution calculator.

Not sure if PF applies to your business at all? PF applies with {{ epf.min_employees }} or more staff. Read PF: who must register.

Staff above ₹25,000: who can join, and who stays

  • Already in PF: they stay in PF, even if their PF wages are above {{ epf.wage_ceiling_text }}. They can't leave because their pay went up.
  • New joiners above {{ epf.wage_ceiling_text }} who were never in PF: they can join PF if you and they both agree in writing.
  • In PF at an old job? Call us to check before you decide.

Staff above the limit still count when you count your staff for the {{ epf.min_employees }}-staff rule.

What to change in your payroll

  1. List your staff. Find everyone whose PF wages are {{ epf.wage_ceiling_text }} a month or less and who is not in PF yet.
  2. Add them to PF. We'll check who must be in PF, so you add the right people. Staff who don't have a UAN (their PF account number) yet get one with a quick face scan on the UMANG app. We guide them.
  3. Update your salary sheet or payroll software. Make sure it uses the {{ epf.wage_ceiling_text }} limit, not {{ epf.wage_ceiling_old_text }}.
  4. Check PF wages against the 50% rule. If basic pay plus DA is less than half the total pay, the difference is added to PF wages.
  5. Pay by the {{ epf.due_day }}th. Each month's PF must be paid by the {{ epf.due_day }}th of the next month. We get the payment slip (challan) ready in time, so you can pay by the {{ epf.due_day }}th.
  6. Tell your staff. Their take-home pay goes down by their PF share. But that money is their own savings. Share our guide on what PF gives employees.

Do you run an IT company? Read PF and ESI for IT companies. It covers the {{ epf.wage_ceiling_text }} limit for IT salaries.

September 2026 salaries: what EPFO's FAQs say

The new limit started on {{ epf.wage_ceiling_from }}, in the middle of the month. The FAQs (answers to common questions) on the new limit from EPFO (the government PF office) say to send one ECR (the monthly list of each employee's wages and PF, sent to the PF office) for September 2026, with PF worked out in two parts:

  • 1 to 16 September: on the old {{ epf.wage_ceiling_old_text }} limit
  • 17 to 30 September: on the new {{ epf.wage_ceiling_text }} limit

The FAQs also say the new {{ epf.wage_ceiling_text }} limit applies to EPS (pension) wages too. Staff who already pay PF on wages above {{ epf.wage_ceiling_text }} don't have to bring it down to {{ epf.wage_ceiling_text }}.

EPFO moved the due date for September 2026 salaries to 31 October 2026. This is for September only. PF on October 2026 salaries is due by 15 November 2026. Call us and we will tell you what to do for your staff.
