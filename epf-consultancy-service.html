Does PF apply to your business?

PF applies if your business has {{ epf.min_employees }} or more employees, in any industry.

Every employee whose PF wages are {{ epf.wage_ceiling_text }} a month or less must be in PF. This limit went up from {{ epf.wage_ceiling_old_text }} to {{ epf.wage_ceiling_text }} on {{ epf.wage_ceiling_from }}. For September 2026 salaries, the rule is not yet clear. Call us.

PF wages are basic pay, DA (dearness allowance) and retaining allowance. They are not total salary. Other allowances usually don't count. But if they are more than half of total pay, the extra part is added to PF wages.

If an employee's PF wages are more than {{ epf.wage_ceiling_text }}, the rules are different. Someone who has never been in PF may join by choice. Anyone who is already a PF member stays a member.

What we do for you

Setting up

  • Register your business with EPFO (the PF office).
  • Register your employees and create their UAN (each employee's PF account number).

Every month

  • Prepare and file your ECR (the monthly PF return) by the {{ epf.due_day }}th of the next month.
  • Add new staff and mark staff who have left.
  • Pay the PF amount for you, if you prefer. Or you pay it yourself using the challan (the PF payment slip) we send.
  • Email you the filing confirmation and the challan.

When something comes up

  • Help your staff with PF transfers, withdrawals and KYC (linking Aadhaar, PAN and bank details).
  • Keep your PF records in order and ready for audits.
  • Support you during PF inspections.
  • Prepare and send letters to the PF office for you.
  • Tell you when PF rules change.
