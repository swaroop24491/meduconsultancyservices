What we do for you

You send us salary details. We do the PF work.

Once

When you start

  • We register your business for PF, if it isn't registered yet.
  • We add your current staff to PF. Staff who already have a UAN (a PF account number for life) keep it. Others get one by a face scan on the UMANG app. We guide them.
  • Already registered? Once you share the documents we need, we file from your next due date.

Monthly

Every month

  • We file your monthly PF return (the ECR) on time.
  • We add staff who join, and mark the date when staff leave.
  • We email you the challan (the PF payment slip). Pay it by the {{ epf.due_day }}th of the next month, or send us the amount and we pay it.

Anytime

When you need help

  • We help your staff move their PF to a new job, or withdraw their PF money.
  • We link their Aadhaar, PAN and bank details to PF (KYC).
  • We keep your PF records ready for checks, and help you during a PF inspection.
  • We write to the PF office for you, and tell you when PF rules change.
