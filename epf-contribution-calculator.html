How the EPF Split Works

The employee contributes 12% (or 10% for reduced-rate establishments) of basic+DA, entirely credited to their own EPF account. The employer also contributes 12%/10%, but it's split: 8.33% goes to EPS (capped at ₹15,000 basic - max ₹1,250/month), the remainder goes to the employee's EPF account, plus a separate 0.5% to EDLI (capped at ₹15,000) and 0.5% admin charges (minimum ₹75/month, calculated on actual basic+DA, uncapped).

The 10% reduced rate applies to establishments with under 20 employees, sick industrial companies, or specific industries (jute, beedi, brick, coir, guar gum).

Under the Employees' Provident Funds Scheme, 2026: for basic+DA above ₹15,000, contribution can be capped at the ₹15,000 ceiling (the mandatory minimum) or voluntarily continued on the full basic+DA if both employer and employee agree.
