How the PF rules work

For your business

Your business must register for PF when it has {{ epf.min_employees }} or more employees, in any industry. Count all your staff, including casual staff, workers a contractor sends and staff on high salaries. Don't count apprentices, the owner or partners. Is your business a company? Call us to check how to count directors.

Once PF applies, it stays, even if your staff later drop below {{ epf.min_employees }}.

For each employee

An employee must be in PF when their PF wages are {{ epf.wage_ceiling_text }} a month or less. This limit went up from {{ epf.wage_ceiling_old_text }} on {{ epf.wage_ceiling_from }}.

Above {{ epf.wage_ceiling_text }}: an employee who was in PF in an old job usually stays in PF. A new employee who was never in PF can join if you both agree. International workers usually must be in PF, whatever their salary; some are left out under an agreement between India and their home country. Call us to check.

Note: This checker is a guide based on the general PF rules. It is not a final answer for your business. Some cases need a closer look, like staff in more than one state, contract workers or apprentices. Call us and we'll check. Checked against the rules as of {{ site.data.facts.tools_checked }}.
