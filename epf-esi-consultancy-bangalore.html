PF and ESI offices for Bangalore businesses

Bangalore has many PF offices (EPFO) and a few ESI offices (ESIC). The office that handles your business depends on your business address.

PF offices in the city include Peenya, Koramangala, Electronic City, K R Puram, Rajarajeshwari Nagar and Yelahanka. ESI has its Karnataka regional office in Binnypet, and sub-regional offices for the Peenya and Bommasandra areas.

Which ESI office covers your area?

ESIC keeps a list of the areas each of its offices covers. Many industrial areas around Bangalore are on these lists:

  • Peenya
  • Jigani
  • Bommasandra and Electronic City
  • Anekal and Attibele
  • Bidadi
