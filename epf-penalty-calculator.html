How PF late payment costs are worked out

PF (also called EPF) is due by the {{ epf.due_day }}th of the next month. If you pay late, EPFO can charge two extra amounts on top of the unpaid PF: interest and damages (a fine for paying late). This calculator works out both, so you know roughly what you may owe.

Interest

Interest is 12% a year on the unpaid amount. It is counted for each day from the due date until the day you pay. It is charged on every late payment.

Damages

Damages are a fine on top of the interest. The rate depends on when the payment was due:

  • Due on or after 14 June 2024: a monthly rate, based on how late you pay. Less than 2 months late: 0.25% a month. 2 months or more, but less than 4: 0.5% a month. 4 months or more: 1% a month. In this estimate, part of a month counts as a full month. Damages can't be more than the unpaid amount.
  • Due before 14 June 2024: a yearly rate that goes up the longer you are late: 5% (less than 2 months), 10% (2 months or more, but less than 4), 15% (4 months or more, but less than 6), 25% (6 months or more). Damages can't be more than the unpaid amount.

Was your PF due before 14 June 2024, and paid after it? The rules for this are not clear. Call us to check.

EPFO decides whether to charge damages, and how much. So the damages figure is an estimate, not a bill.

Note: This calculator gives an estimate. It is not the final amount. EPFO decides the final amount after it looks at your case. Checked against the rules as of {{ site.data.facts.tools_checked }}.
