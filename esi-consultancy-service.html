What we do for you

You send us salary details. We do the ESI work.

Once

When you start

  • We register your business for ESI, if it isn't registered yet.
  • We add your current staff to ESI. Staff who already have an IP number (each employee's ESI number, which stays the same for life) keep it. We create one for the others.
  • Already registered? Once you share the documents we need, we file from your next due date.

Monthly

Every month

  • We file your monthly ESI details with ESIC (the ESI office) on time.
  • We add staff who join, and mark the date when staff leave.
  • We email you the challan (the ESI payment slip). Pay it by the {{ esi.due_day }}th of the next month, or send us the amount and we pay it.

Anytime

When you need help

  • We help your staff with ESI claims, like sickness or maternity benefit.
  • We keep their ESI details right: Aadhaar, bank account, name and date of birth.
  • We keep your ESI records ready for checks, and help you during an ESI inspection.
  • We write to the ESI office for you, and tell you when ESI rules change.
