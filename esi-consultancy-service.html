Does ESI apply to your business?

ESI applies if your business has {{ esi.min_employees }} or more employees. This is the same in every state. ESI does not apply to seasonal factories.

Count all your staff, including casual workers and workers a contractor sends to work for you. Once ESI applies, it stays, even if your staff drop below {{ esi.min_employees }}. For some risky kinds of work, ESI can apply even with 1 employee. Call us to check.

Every employee whose ESI wages are {{ esi.wage_ceiling_text }} a month or less must be in ESI. For a person with a disability, the limit is {{ esi.wage_ceiling_pwd_text }}.

ESI wages are the month's pay without some items, like HRA (house rent allowance) and overtime. If those items are more than half the pay, part of them is added back. We work this out for you.

ESI works in two six-month periods: {{ esi.contribution_periods_text }}. If an employee's pay goes above {{ esi.wage_ceiling_text }} during a period, they stay in ESI until it ends. You keep paying ESI for them until then.

What we do for you

Setting up (one-time)

  • Register your business with ESIC (the ESI office).
  • Register your employees and create their IP number (each employee's ESI number; IP means "insured person").

Every month

  • File your monthly ESI details on the ESIC website by the {{ esi.due_day }}th of the next month.
  • Register new staff when they join, and mark staff who have left.
  • Pay the ESI amount for you, if you prefer. Or you pay it yourself using the challan (the ESI payment slip) we send.
  • Email you the filing confirmation and the challan.

When something comes up

  • Help your staff with ESI claims, like sickness or maternity benefit.
  • Help your staff keep their ESI details right (Aadhaar, bank account, name and date of birth).
  • Keep your ESI records in order and ready for audits.
  • Support you during ESI inspections.
  • Prepare and send letters to the ESI office for you.
  • Tell you when ESI rules change.
