How the ESI Split Works

Total ESI contribution is 4% of gross wages - 3.25% from the employer and 0.75% from the employee. It applies only if gross wages are ≤ ₹21,000/month (≤ ₹25,000 for a person with disability).

Low-wage exemption

If the employee's average daily wage is ₹176 or less, their own 0.75% share is exempted - the employer must still pay the 3.25% share.
