How ESI is worked out

ESI is {{ esi.total_rate_pct }}% of ESI wages each month. The employee pays {{ esi.employee_rate_pct }}%, which you take from their salary. You pay {{ esi.employer_rate_pct }}%.

ESI is paid for employees whose ESI wages are {{ esi.wage_ceiling_text }} a month or less ({{ esi.wage_ceiling_pwd_text }} for a person with a disability).

ESI wages are the month's pay without some items, like HRA (house rent allowance) and overtime. If those items are more than half the pay, part of them is added back.

Staff on very low daily pay don't pay their own {{ esi.employee_rate_pct }}% share. You still pay your {{ esi.employer_rate_pct }}%. Call us to check if this applies.

ESI is due by the {{ esi.due_day }}th of the next month.

Note: This calculator gives an estimate. The final figure can differ slightly because of rounding. Checked against the rules as of {{ site.data.facts.tools_checked }}.
