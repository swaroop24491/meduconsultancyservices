How ESI Eligibility Works

Establishment-level coverage

ESI registration is mandatory for every establishment with 10 or more employees, in every state, under the Code on Social Security, 2020. Seasonal factories are not covered. Some hazardous work must register even with 1 employee. Like EPF, coverage is permanent once triggered - dropping below 10 employees later does not remove it.

Employee-level coverage

An employee is covered if their gross monthly wages are ₹21,000 or less (₹25,000 or less for a person with disability). Wages here means all pay for the month, leaving out HRA, overtime, bonus, commission and travel allowance. If those left-out items are more than half of total pay, the part above half is added back. If wages cross the ceiling partway through a contribution period (April–September or October–March), coverage continues until that period ends.

Low-wage exemption

If an employee's average daily wage is ₹176 or less, their own 0.75% share is exempted - the employer must still pay their 3.25% share.
