How ESI late payment costs are worked out

ESI is due by the {{ esi.due_day }}th of the next month. If you pay late, ESIC can charge two extra amounts on top of the unpaid ESI: interest and damages (a fine for paying late). This calculator works out both, so you know roughly what you may owe.

Interest

Interest is 12% a year on the unpaid amount. It is counted for each day from the due date until the day you pay.

Damages

Damages are a fine on top of the interest. ESIC decides whether to charge them. The yearly rate goes up the longer you are late: 5% (less than 2 months), 10% (2 months or more, but less than 4), 15% (4 months or more, but less than 6), 25% (6 months or more). Damages can't be more than the unpaid amount.

Note: This calculator gives an estimate. It is not the final amount. ESIC decides whether to charge damages, and the final amount, after it looks at your case. Checked against the rules as of {{ site.data.facts.tools_checked }}.
