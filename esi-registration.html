Does your business need ESI registration?

  • {{ esi.min_employees }} employees or more
  • {{ esi.wage_ceiling_text }} a month or less in ESI wages: these staff must join

ESI (Employees' State Insurance) gives your staff medical care, and pay when they can't work because of sickness, injury or maternity. Your business must register for ESI when it has {{ esi.min_employees }} or more employees. This is the same in every state. ESI does not apply to seasonal factories.

Count all your staff:

  • temporary and casual staff
  • workers a contractor sends to work for you
  • staff on high salaries

Don't count: apprentices (trainees under the Apprentices Act), the owner of the business, or its partners. Is your business a company? Call us to check how to count directors.

Once ESI applies, it stays. It does not stop if your staff later drop below {{ esi.min_employees }}.

For some risky kinds of work, ESI can apply even with 1 employee. Call us to check.

Staff with ESI wages up to {{ esi.wage_ceiling_text }} a month must be in ESI. ESI wages are the month's pay without some items, like HRA (house rent allowance) and overtime. We check this for you.

After registration

ESI must be paid every month, by the {{ esi.due_day }}th of the next month.

Each month, the employee pays 0.75% of their ESI wages, and you pay 3.25%. That is 4% in total. You take the employee's part from their salary. Staff on very low daily pay don't pay their part, but you still pay yours.

We can do this work for you every month. You send us salary details. We file your monthly ESI details with ESIC and email you the challan (the ESI payment slip). Pay it by the {{ esi.due_day }}th of the next month, or send us the amount and we pay it.
