Does your business need ESI registration?

Your business must register for ESI when it has {{ esi.min_employees }} or more employees. This is the same in every state. Seasonal factories are not covered.

Count all your staff. This includes temporary and casual staff, and workers a contractor sends to work for you. Count staff on high salaries too. Apprentices (trainees under the Apprentices Act) are not counted. Owners and partners are not counted either.

Once registered, ESI stays. It does not stop if your staff later drop below {{ esi.min_employees }}.

For some risky kinds of work, ESI can apply even with 1 employee. Call us to check.

Every employee whose ESI wages are {{ esi.wage_ceiling_text }} a month or less must then be in ESI. For a person with a disability, the limit is {{ esi.wage_ceiling_pwd_text }}.

ESI wages are the month's pay, but some items are left out, like HRA (house rent allowance) and overtime. If these left-out items are more than half the pay, part of them counts again. We work this out for you.

What we need from you

We tell you which documents we need when you call. The list depends on your type of business. You send them to us by email. No office visits needed.

What we do

Register your business with ESIC (the ESI office)

Your business gets an ESI code number (17 digits). This is your business's ESI account number. Once we have all your documents, this takes just a few hours.

Register your staff

We register each employee on the ESIC website and create their IP number (each employee's ESI number; IP means "insured person"). If someone already has an IP number from an old job, we use that one.

After registration

After registration, ESI must be paid every month, by the {{ esi.due_day }}th of the next month.

We can do this for you. Each month, you send salary details and we file. We email you the confirmation and the challan (the ESI payment slip).
