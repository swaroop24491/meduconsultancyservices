Does your business need ESI registration?

Your business must register for ESI when it has {{ esi.min_employees }} or more employees. This is the same in every state. ESI does not apply to seasonal factories.

Count all your staff:

  • temporary and casual staff
  • workers a contractor sends to work for you
  • staff on high salaries

Don't count: apprentices (trainees under the Apprentices Act), the owner of the business, or its partners. Is your business a company? Call us to check how to count directors.

Once ESI applies, it stays. It does not stop if your staff later drop below {{ esi.min_employees }}.

For some risky kinds of work, ESI can apply even with 1 employee. Call us to check.

Every employee whose ESI wages are {{ esi.wage_ceiling_text }} a month or less must then be in ESI. For a person with a disability, the limit is {{ esi.wage_ceiling_pwd_text }}.

ESI wages are the month's pay without some items, like HRA (house rent allowance) and overtime. If those items are more than half the pay, part of them is added back. We work this out for you.

What we do

We tell you which documents we need when you call. The list depends on your type of business. You send them to us by email. No office visits needed. We register businesses in Mangalore (Mangaluru), Udupi and Bangalore.

Register your business with ESIC (the ESI office)

Your business gets an ESI code number (17 digits). This is your business's ESI account number. Once we have all your documents, this takes just a few hours.

Register your staff

We register each employee on the ESIC website and create their IP number (each employee's ESI number; IP means "insured person"). If someone already has an IP number from an old job, we use that one.

After registration

ESI must be paid every month, by the {{ esi.due_day }}th of the next month.

Each month, the employee pays 0.75% of their ESI wages, and you pay 3.25%. That is 4% in total. You take the employee's part from their salary. Staff on very low daily pay don't pay their part, but you still pay yours.

Your staff use their IP number to get ESI medical care, and to claim cash help, for example when they are too sick to work. We help them with claims.

Each month, you send us salary details. We file, and email you the confirmation and the challan (the ESI payment slip).
