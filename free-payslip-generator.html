        What a payslip shows

        A payslip usually shows the company's name, the employee's name and ID, the month, the paid days, each earning and each deduction, and the net pay (what the employee gets).

        PF (Provident Fund, also called EPF) gives your staff savings and a pension. ESI (Employees' State Insurance) gives your staff medical care and sick pay. If the rules cover an employee, their share of PF and ESI is taken from their salary and shown as a deduction.
