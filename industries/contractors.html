How PF and ESI apply to your workers

As a contractor, you are the employer of your workers. PF (Provident Fund, also called EPF) applies once you have {{ epf.min_employees }} or more employees. ESI (Employees' State Insurance) applies once you have {{ esi.min_employees }} or more. Then you register your workers and pay both every month. Smaller contractor? Call us to check.

Every month

Workers join and leave every month

Most new workers need a UAN (their PF account number) and an IP number (their ESI number). A worker with a UAN from an old job keeps it. Others get one by a face scan on the UMANG app (the government's mobile app). We guide them.

Each worker who leaves must be marked as left, with the date. If one is missed, their records go wrong.

Challans

Your clients check your PF and ESI

The company that hires you is called the main employer (in law, the "principal employer"). If you don't pay PF and ESI for your workers, the main employer can be asked to pay. So many clients ask for your challans (PF and ESI payment slips) before they clear your bills. Filing on time helps you get paid on time.

Every site

Workers at many sites

Count and cover every worker, at every site. Workers at a far site are still your employees.

When workers don't want to join

Many workers want full cash pay and ask to skip PF or ESI. If the law covers them, they must join. You can't leave them out.

Tell them what they get. PF builds savings, a pension and life insurance. It moves with them to their next job through their UAN. ESI gives them and their family free medical care, and cash help if they are hurt at work.
