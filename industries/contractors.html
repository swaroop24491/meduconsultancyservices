How PF and ESI apply to your workers

As a contractor, you are the employer of your workers. PF (Provident Fund) applies once you have {{ epf.min_employees }} or more employees. ESI (Employees' State Insurance) applies once you have {{ esi.min_employees }} or more. Then you register your workers and pay both every month. Smaller contractor? Call us to check.

Workers join and leave every month

Each new worker needs a UAN (their PF account number) and an IP number (their ESI number). Each worker who leaves must be marked as left. If one is missed, their records don't match and problems build up.

Your clients check your PF and ESI

The company that hires you is called the main employer. If you don't pay PF and ESI for your workers, the main employer can be asked to pay. So many clients ask for your challans (PF and ESI payment slips) before they clear your bills. Filing on time helps you get paid on time.

Workers at many sites

Count and cover every worker, at every site. Workers at a far site are still your employees.

New workers and their UAN

New workers get their UAN with a quick face scan on the UMANG app. We guide them. If a worker already has a UAN from an old job, we use that one.

When workers don't want to join

Many workers want full cash pay and ask to skip PF or ESI. If they are eligible, they can't opt out, and you can't leave them out.

Tell them what they get. PF builds savings, a pension and life insurance. It moves with them to their next job through their UAN. ESI gives them and their family free medical care, and cash help if they are hurt at work.

What we handle for you

  • Register your business for PF and ESI, if you are new to it.
  • Every month, add new workers to PF and ESI, and mark the ones who left.
  • Guide new workers through the UAN face scan. We create their IP number.
  • File PF and ESI for every site, every month, before the due date (the {{ epf.due_day }}th of the next month).
  • Email you the confirmation and challans to share with your clients.
  • Keep your records ready for PF and ESI inspections.
