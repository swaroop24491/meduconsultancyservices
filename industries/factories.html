How PF and ESI apply to your workers

PF (Provident Fund, also called EPF) applies once you have {{ epf.min_employees }} or more employees. ESI (Employees' State Insurance) applies once you have {{ esi.min_employees }} or more.

Count everyone

Count casual workers too. Also count workers a contractor sends to you. They all count towards the {{ esi.min_employees }} and {{ epf.min_employees }} limits. Leaving them out is one of the most common mistakes. Once PF or ESI applies, it stays, even if your staff drop below the limit.

ESI wages and overtime

Many factory workers earn {{ esi.wage_ceiling_text }} a month or less in ESI wages, so they must be in ESI. Overtime is left out of ESI wages. But if HRA (house rent allowance), overtime and similar items are more than half the pay, part of them is added back. We work this out each month.

Minimum wage changes

When minimum wages go up, pay goes up. So PF and ESI amounts go up too. We update them for you.

Inspections

Inspectors usually ask for wage registers, records of contract workers, and proof that your contractors paid PF and ESI. Keep these ready.

When workers don't want to join

Some workers ask to skip PF or ESI to take home more pay. If the law covers them, they must join.

Tell them what they get. ESI gives them and their family free medical care, and cash help if they are hurt at work or fall sick. PF builds savings, a pension and life insurance.

What we handle for you

  • Register your factory for PF and ESI, if you are new to it.
  • Count every worker correctly, including casual and contract workers.
  • Add every new worker to PF and ESI. We guide them to get their UAN (their PF account number) with a quick face scan on the UMANG app (the government's mobile app). We create their IP number (their ESI number).
  • Mark workers who left, and update PF and ESI when wages change.
  • Each month, you send us salary details. We file PF and ESI before the due date (the {{ epf.due_day }}th of the next month) and email you the confirmation and challans (the payment slips).
  • Keep your PF and ESI records ready for inspections, and support you during them.
