How PF and ESI apply to your staff

PF (Provident Fund) applies once you have {{ epf.min_employees }} or more employees. ESI (Employees' State Insurance) applies once you have {{ esi.min_employees }} or more.

The new {{ epf.wage_ceiling_text }} PF limit

From {{ epf.wage_ceiling_from }}, PF is a must for employees whose PF wages are {{ epf.wage_ceiling_text }} a month or less. It was {{ epf.wage_ceiling_old_text }} before. PF wages are mainly basic pay plus DA (dearness allowance), not the full salary. So many more of your staff now must be in PF.

The 50% wages rule

Many IT salaries have a low basic pay and large allowances. If other allowances are more than half the salary, part of them also counts as PF wages. Salary structures may need to change. We work this out for you.

"Can I stop my PF?"

An employee who is already in PF stays in PF, even when their salary goes above {{ epf.wage_ceiling_text }}. For a new joiner above {{ epf.wage_ceiling_text }}, check if they were in PF at an old job. If they were, they usually stay in PF. Call us to check.

ESI

ESI applies to your company once you have {{ esi.min_employees }} or more employees. But only staff with ESI wages of {{ esi.wage_ceiling_text }} a month or less ({{ esi.wage_ceiling_pwd_text }} for a person with a disability) are in ESI, like office support staff.

When staff don't want to join

Some employees ask to leave PF to take home more pay. If they are in PF, they can't simply stop.

Tell them what they get. PF builds savings with interest, a pension and life insurance. It moves with them to the next job through their UAN (their PF account number).

What we handle for you

  • Register your company for PF and ESI, if you are new to it.
  • Check which staff now come under PF with the {{ epf.wage_ceiling_text }} limit.
  • Work out PF wages with the 50% rule, each month.
  • Check new joiners' past PF membership.
  • File PF and ESI every month, before the due date (the {{ epf.due_day }}th of the next month).
