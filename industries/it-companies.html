How PF and ESI apply to your staff

PF (Provident Fund, also called EPF) applies once you have {{ epf.min_employees }} or more employees. ESI (Employees' State Insurance) applies once you have {{ esi.min_employees }} or more.

{{ epf.wage_ceiling_text }}

The new {{ epf.wage_ceiling_text }} PF limit

From {{ epf.wage_ceiling_from }}, PF is a must for employees whose PF wages are {{ epf.wage_ceiling_text }} a month or less. It was {{ epf.wage_ceiling_old_text }} before. PF wages are mainly basic pay plus DA (dearness allowance), not the full salary. So many more of your staff must now be in PF.

50%

The 50% wages rule

Many IT salaries have low basic pay and big allowances. If these allowances are more than half the salary, part of them now counts as PF wages too. So PF wages, and the PF you pay, can go up. We calculate the right PF wages for you each month.

Stays in PF

"Can I stop my PF?"

An employee who is already in PF stays in PF, even when their salary goes above {{ epf.wage_ceiling_text }}. Is a new joiner earning above {{ epf.wage_ceiling_text }}? Check if they had PF at an old job. If yes, they usually stay in PF. Call us to check.

ESI: only staff with ESI wages of {{ esi.wage_ceiling_text }} a month or less are in ESI ({{ esi.wage_ceiling_pwd_text }} for a person with a disability). In IT companies, this is usually office support staff.

When staff don't want to join

Some employees ask to leave PF to take home more pay. If they are in PF, they can't simply stop.

Tell them what they get. PF builds savings with interest, a pension and life insurance. It moves with them to the next job through their UAN (their PF account number).
