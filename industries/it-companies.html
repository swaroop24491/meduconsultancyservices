How PF and ESI apply to your staff

PF (Provident Fund, also called EPF) applies once you have {{ epf.min_employees }} or more employees. ESI (Employees' State Insurance) applies once you have {{ esi.min_employees }} or more.

The new {{ epf.wage_ceiling_text }} PF limit

From {{ epf.wage_ceiling_from }}, PF is a must for employees whose PF wages are {{ epf.wage_ceiling_text }} a month or less. It was {{ epf.wage_ceiling_old_text }} before. PF wages are mainly basic pay plus DA (dearness allowance), not the full salary. So many more of your staff must now be in PF.

The 50% wages rule

Many IT salaries have low basic pay and big allowances. If these allowances are more than half the salary, part of them now counts as PF wages too. So PF wages, and the PF you pay, can go up. We work out the right PF wages for you each month.

"Can I stop my PF?"

An employee who is already in PF stays in PF, even when their salary goes above {{ epf.wage_ceiling_text }}. Is a new joiner earning above {{ epf.wage_ceiling_text }}? Check if they had PF at an old job. If yes, they usually stay in PF. Call us to check.

ESI

ESI applies to your company once you have {{ esi.min_employees }} or more employees. But only staff earning {{ esi.wage_ceiling_text }} a month or less in ESI wages are in ESI. For a person with a disability, the limit is {{ esi.wage_ceiling_pwd_text }}. In IT companies, this is usually office support staff.

When staff don't want to join

Some employees ask to leave PF to take home more pay. If they are in PF, they can't simply stop.

Tell them what they get. PF builds savings with interest, a pension and life insurance. It moves with them to the next job through their UAN (their PF account number).

What we handle for you

  • Register your company for PF and ESI, if you are new to it.
  • Check which staff now come under PF with the {{ epf.wage_ceiling_text }} limit.
  • Work out PF wages with the 50% rule, each month.
  • Check new joiners' past PF membership.
  • Add every new employee to PF, and to ESI if they qualify. We guide them to get their UAN (their PF account number) with a quick face scan on the UMANG app (the government's mobile app).
  • Each month, you send us salary details. We file PF and ESI before the due date (the {{ epf.due_day }}th of the next month) and email you the confirmation and challans (the payment slips).
  • Keep your PF and ESI records ready for inspections.
