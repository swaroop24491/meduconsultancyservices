How PF and ESI apply to your staff

PF (Provident Fund) applies once you have {{ epf.min_employees }} or more employees. ESI (Employees' State Insurance) applies once you have {{ esi.min_employees }} or more. A private school follows the same rules as any other business.

CBSE schools

CBSE's affiliation rules ask schools to follow PF, ESI and labour laws for their staff. It can come up when you apply for affiliation or renew it.

Teaching and non-teaching staff

Both are covered. That includes drivers, helpers, cleaners, security and office staff. Non-teaching staff often earn {{ esi.wage_ceiling_text }} a month or less in ESI wages, so they must be in ESI.

Part-time and temporary staff

Regular part-time and temporary staff can be covered too. Call us to check your staff.

When staff don't want to join

In schools, staff often ask to skip PF or ESI. If they are eligible, they can't opt out.

Tell them what they get. PF builds savings for later, a pension and life insurance. ESI gives them and their family free medical care, and cash help when they are sick or on maternity leave.

What we handle for you

  • Register your school for PF and ESI, if you are new to it.
  • Add every teacher and staff member to PF and ESI. We guide them to get their UAN (their PF account number) with a quick face scan on the UMANG app. We create their IP number (their ESI number).
  • File PF and ESI every month, before the due date (the {{ epf.due_day }}th of the next month).
  • Email you the confirmation and the challans (payment slips), so your records are ready for CBSE and inspections.
  • Help your staff with their PF and ESI details.
