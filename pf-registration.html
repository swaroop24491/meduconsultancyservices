Does your business need PF registration?

Your business must register for PF when it has {{ epf.min_employees }} or more employees, in any industry.

Count all your staff. This includes temporary and casual staff, and workers a contractor sends to work for you. Count staff on high salaries too. Apprentices (trainees under the Apprentices Act) are not counted. Owners and partners are not counted either.

Once registered, PF stays. It does not stop if your staff later drop below {{ epf.min_employees }}.

Every employee whose PF wages are {{ epf.wage_ceiling_text }} a month or less must then be in PF. For September 2026 salaries, the PF office has not yet said which limit to use. Call us and we'll tell you what to do.

PF wages are mainly basic pay plus DA (dearness allowance). It is not the full salary. If other allowances are more than half the salary, part of them also counts. We work this out for you.

Not sure? Check with our PF eligibility checker⁠{% include icon.html name="arrow" size="18" %}

What we need from you

We tell you which documents we need when you call. The list depends on your type of business. You send them to us by email. No office visits needed.

What we do

Register your business with EPFO (the PF office)

Your business gets a PF code number (the PF office calls it the establishment code). This is your business's PF account number. Once we have all your documents, this takes just a few hours.

Register your staff

Each employee needs a UAN (their PF account number for life). New staff get it with a quick face scan on the UMANG app. We guide them. If someone already has a UAN from an old job, we use that one.

After registration

Once you are registered, PF must be paid every month, by the {{ epf.due_day }}th of the next month.

We can do this for you. Each month, you send salary details and we file. We email you the confirmation and the challan (the PF payment slip).
