Does your business need PF registration?

  • {{ epf.min_employees }} employees or more
  • {{ epf.wage_ceiling_text }} a month or less in PF wages: these staff must join

PF (Provident Fund, also called EPF) gives your staff savings and a pension. Your business must register for PF when it has {{ epf.min_employees }} or more employees, in any industry.

Count all your staff:

  • temporary and casual staff
  • workers a contractor sends to work for you
  • staff on high salaries

Don't count: apprentices (trainees under the Apprentices Act), the owner of the business, or its partners. Is your business a company? Call us to check how to count directors.

Once PF applies, it stays. It does not stop if your staff later drop below {{ epf.min_employees }}.

Staff with PF wages (mainly basic pay plus DA, dearness allowance) up to {{ epf.wage_ceiling_text }} a month must be in PF. We check this for you.

What we do for you

No office visits needed. We register businesses in Mangalore (Mangaluru) and Bangalore.

First

We check and ask

  • We check if PF applies to your business, and count your staff with you.
  • We tell you which documents we need. The list depends on your type of business.
  • You send them to us by email.

Next

We register your business

  • We register your business with EPFO (the PF office).
  • Once we have all your documents, it takes just a few hours.
  • You get a PF code number for your business. The PF office calls it the establishment code.

Then

We add your staff

  • We add your staff to PF, if the rules cover them.
  • Staff who already have a UAN (a PF account number for life) keep it.
  • Others get one by a face scan on the UMANG app (the government's mobile app). We guide them.

After registration

PF must be paid every month, by the {{ epf.due_day }}th of the next month.

Each month, the employee pays 12% of their PF wages, and you pay 12%. You take the employee's part from their salary. Part of your share goes to the employee's pension (EPS, the Employees' Pension Scheme). We work out the exact amounts.

We can do this work for you every month. You send us salary details. We file your monthly PF return (the ECR: the monthly list of each employee's wages and PF, sent to the PF office) and email you the challan (the PF payment slip). Pay it by the {{ epf.due_day }}th of the next month, or send us the amount and we pay it.
