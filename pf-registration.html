Does your business need PF registration?

Your business must register for PF when it has {{ epf.min_employees }} or more employees, in any industry.

Count all your staff:

  • temporary and casual staff
  • workers a contractor sends to work for you
  • staff on high salaries

Don't count: apprentices (trainees under the Apprentices Act), the owner of the business, or its partners. Is your business a company? Call us to check how to count directors.

Once PF applies, it stays. It does not stop if your staff later drop below {{ epf.min_employees }}.

Every employee whose PF wages are {{ epf.wage_ceiling_text }} a month or less must then be in PF. This limit went up from {{ epf.wage_ceiling_old_text }} to {{ epf.wage_ceiling_text }} on {{ epf.wage_ceiling_from }}. For September 2026 salaries, the PF office has not yet said which limit to use. Call us and we'll tell you what to do.

PF wages are mainly basic pay plus DA (dearness allowance). It is not the full salary. If other allowances are more than half the salary, part of them also counts. We work this out for you.

Not sure? Check with our PF eligibility checker⁠{% include icon.html name="arrow" size="18" %}

What we do

We tell you which documents we need when you call. The list depends on your type of business. You send them to us by email. No office visits needed. We register businesses in Mangalore (Mangaluru), Udupi and Bangalore.

Register your business with EPFO (the PF office)

Your business gets a PF code number (the PF office calls it the establishment code). This is your business's PF account number. Once we have all your documents, this takes just a few hours.

Register your staff

Each employee needs a UAN (their PF account number for life). New staff get it with a quick face scan on the UMANG app. We guide them. If someone already has a UAN from an old job, we use that one.

After registration

PF must be paid every month, by the {{ epf.due_day }}th of the next month.

Each month, the employee pays 12% of their PF wages, and you pay 12%. You take the employee's part from their salary. Part of your share goes to the employee's pension (EPS, the Employees' Pension Scheme). We work out the exact amounts.

Your staff use their UAN to check their PF balance, and to move their PF when they change jobs. We help them with transfers, withdrawals and KYC (linking Aadhaar, PAN and bank details).

Each month, you send us salary details. We file, and email you the confirmation and the challan (the PF payment slip).
