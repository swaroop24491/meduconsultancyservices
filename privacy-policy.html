Privacy policy

This website has no forms, and we do not sell your information. This page explains what the website collects, and why.

Last updated {{ page.last_modified | date: "%-d %B %Y" }}

Who runs this website

Medu Consultancy (Medu Consultancy Services), Mangaluru, runs this website.

What we collect

This website has no forms. We do not ask for your name, email or any financial details here.

Like most websites, it notes a few things about your visit: your internet address (IP address), your browser, your device, and which pages you open. It also counts taps on our phone number. Google Analytics, a free Google tool, does this for us. We also use Google Search Console, another Google tool, to see which searches bring people here.

The Excel to Text converter works inside your browser. Your file is not sent to us or anyone else.

How we use it

We use this to count visits, to see which pages people open, and to make the website better.

Sharing and security

We do not sell or rent your information. Google receives the information above for analytics. The website also loads fonts from Google, so Google may see your IP address when a page loads.

The map on our Contact page also comes from Google. It loads only if you tap "Show the map". Until then, nothing is sent to Google for the map.

No website can be fully secure.

Cookies

The website uses cookies. These are small files saved in your browser to count visits. You can block or delete cookies in your browser settings.

Links to other websites

Our website may link to other websites. We are not responsible for how they use your information.

Changes to this page

We may change this page. The date at the top shows when it last changed.
