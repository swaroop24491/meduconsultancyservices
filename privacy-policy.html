How this website uses information

This page explains what information this website collects and how we use it. The website is run by Medu Consultancy (Medu Consultancy Services), Mangaluru.

What we collect

This website has no forms. We do not ask for your name, email or any financial details here.

Like most websites, it notes a few things about your visit: your internet address (IP address), your browser, your device, and which pages you open. Google Analytics, a free Google tool, does this for us. We also use Google Search Console, another Google tool, to see which searches bring people here.

How we use it

We use this to count visits, to see which pages people open, and to make the website better.

Sharing and security

We do not sell or rent your information. Google receives the information above for analytics. The website also loads fonts from Google, so Google may see your IP address when a page loads. No website can be fully secure.

Cookies

The website uses cookies. These are small files saved in your browser to count visits. You can block or delete cookies in your browser settings.

Links to other websites

Our website may link to other websites. We are not responsible for how they use your information.
